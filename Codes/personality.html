Critical Reflection: LLMs as a Tool, Not a Crutch

Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced AI systems built on deep neural networks designed to process, understand and generate human-like text (“Introduction to Large Language Model (LLM)”, 2026) The topic of these systems ever since creation has been controversial purely beacaue it could make or break the education system the and working world, but i believe it depends on how the users use it. “LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch” A crutch versus s a tool, using LLMs as a crutch means youre soley dependent on the system and could not function without it even if you tried whereas a tool you would use it to help or enhance what you already know or have done.

The Crutch, “Productive struggle is about working at the edge of your ability — tasks that are challenging but achievable,” Hamsa Bastani explained. “AI assistance that makes tasks too easy pushes you out of that learning zone. You’re no longer practicing at the level where skill development happens.” (“When Does AI Assistance Undermine Learning?”, 2026). Relying on the systems to do almost if not everything does not aid in learning and growth it pose a harm to learning. Students as opposed to figuring out a challenging question would just resort to these systems, which is very unhealthy. Excessive reliance on AI risks weakening interpersonal relationships, social bonds, and employee well-being, as people may increasingly interact with AI rather than colleagues, leading to a loss of meaning and purpose at work. (“The human side of AI: The growing risks of ubiquitous use of AI on talent”, 2026).

The Tool, using AI systems as a tool is not an issue as you would just be using it to enhance and guide you through completing tasks you already know how to do. It can be used to ensure the work you are producing is up to your standard. To help make informed decisions, Guide you throughout studies and explain complex topics. This ensures you are not over reliant on the systems.“Most people right now are using AI as a crutch,” Stewart said. “But to be equipped for the future job market, people need to have a competency-based knowledge of AI. Not just use Chat GPT, but make it an effective tool.”(Wachs, 2024)

In Coclusion AI systems should not replace human Intelligence but amplify it, AI is constantly changing. Education can keep up. “Most people right now are using AI as a crutch,” Stewart said. “But to be equipped for the future job market, people need to have a competency-based knowledge of AI. Not just use Chat GPT, but make it an effective tool.”.(Wachs, 2024)No person who is in training, learning or in a field that requires you to constantly learn use LLMs as a crutch.
