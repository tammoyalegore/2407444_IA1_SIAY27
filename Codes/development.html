Book Study: The Richest Man in Babylon by George S. Clason

Jump to Section:

  • Seven Cures for a Lean Purse
  • Five Laws of Gold

The Richest Man In Babylon by George S, Clason, is a text which provides readers with step by step guidance on financial wisdom using different parables. It tells the story of Arkad “The Richest Man in Babylon” who was once a poor scribe teaching the population of men in Babylon how to gain riches. The Two Key chapters of the text are “ Seven Cures for a Lean Purse” which teaches you how to establish wealth and money and “Five Laws of Gold” which teaches you how to conserve and invest wealth.

Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse was insightful, heres my understanding, 1.“Start Thy Purse to Fattening.” This would mean to save 10% of your earnings and ensure its done before anything else. 2. “Control Thy Expenditures.” Ensure you know the difference between wants and needs and live with your means. 3. “Make thy Gold Multiply” This is basically put investing and compounding and watch the amount you initially invested grow. 4. “Guard thy treasures from lost” Arkad is advising that taking a huge risk for big returns is not worth it and be careful with investments before approaching them because you could lose instead of gain. 5.“Make of Thy Dwelling a Profitable Investment. This would be Arkad suggesting that instead of renting we should buy. 6.“Insure a Future Income. Arkad instructs us to prepare for the retirement stage as you won’t always be young. 7.“Increase Thy Ability to Earn.” This is ensuring that you keep developing your skills and being open-minded will assure you riches.

Five Laws of Gold

As for The Five Laws of Gold this chapter was just as interesting, 1.The First Law of Gold-"Gold cometh gladly and in increasing quantity to any man who will put by not less than one-tenth of his earnings to create an estate for his future and that of his family." this is similar to the first Cure which is saving an amount of your earnings which helps you build a foundation 2. The Second Law of Gold- "Gold laboreth diligently and contentedly for the wise owner who finds for it profitable employment, multiplying even as the flocks of the field." this also trails back our 3rd cure which is investing and compounding, siting on the money you’ve saved won’t promise you wealth but investing and compounding will it’s basically making your money work for you. 3. The Third Law of Gold- “Gold clingeth to the protection of the cautious owner who invests it under the advice of men wise in its handling." there seems to be a connection to each cure with the laws as this again links to cure 4 which teaches us not to take big risks, we should pay keen attention to details in order to not incur any losses. 4. The Fourth Law of Gold- “Gold slippeth away from the man who invests it in businesses or purposes with which he is not familiar or which are not approved by those skilled in its keep.” as previously stated big risks can incur big losses and investing in things you don’t understand or dosen’t seem to make sense can cause a huge loss especially if you’re not certain. 5. The Fifth Law of Gold- “Gold flees the man who would force it to impossible earnings or who followeth the alluring advice of tricksters and schemers or who trusts it to his own inexperience and romantic desires in investment.”, Protect your self from getting scammed as no wealth or riches come overnight.
